  * <info>UX_IMAGE_DSN</info> is commented out in <info>.env</info>, so images keep their original URL
    in dev. Set it for production, in <info>.env.prod.local</info> or as a Symfony secret, with the
    domain your Cloudflare zone proxies as host, the one your original images are served from.

  * Image transformations must be enabled on that zone before this works.

  * <fg=blue>Read</> the documentation at <comment>https://github.com/symfony/ux/blob/3.x/src/Image/src/Bridge/Cloudflare/README.md</>
