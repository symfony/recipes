  * The recipe adds an <info>ipx</info> service to <info>compose.yaml</info>. <info>UX_IMAGE_DSN</info> in <info>.env</info> points at it over plain HTTP. With the Symfony CLI, <info>IPX_HOST</info> and <info>IPX_PORT</info> come from the port published in <info>compose.override.yaml</info>.

  * IPX has no URL signing. <info>IPX_ALLOWED_MODIFIERS</info> in <info>compose.yaml</info> only allows the common modifiers. Add each extra operation you pass.
